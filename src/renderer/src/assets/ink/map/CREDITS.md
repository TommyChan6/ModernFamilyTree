# Map painting sprites — sources & licences

Every sprite here was cut from a photograph of a Song / Yuan dynasty landscape
painting hosted on Wikimedia Commons. The paintings are many centuries old and
in the public domain, and the faithful photographic reproductions are public
domain too (Commons `{{PD-Art}}` / PD-old; the Met's photograph is also released
under its Open Access programme, CC0). The silk/paper ground was keyed out, the
foot of each crop dissolved into mist, and the result downscaled to ≤ 512 px.
The processing script is `scripts/inkMapSprites/process.py`.
